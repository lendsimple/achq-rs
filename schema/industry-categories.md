# Industry categories

## Associate your accounts with industry types

When activating a merchant account via the [Onboarding](https://developers.achq.com/docs/merchant-onboarding) API, you are asked to define both an "Industry category" and "Specific industry."  These pairings help ACHQ to better classify your portfolio and automatically assign [merchant category codes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Merchant_category_code) and [NAICS codes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_American_Industry_Classification_System) behind the scenes.  Acceptable values for each pair can be found in the table below.

> 🚧 If you are unclear on how to classify your customers, or if your market is not accounted for, please let us know.
